PackiPals

Takeout food loses heat fast on the way home. I framed a How Might We question around keeping it warm, researched how heat escapes takeout containers, and compared insulating materials. After two rounds of paper-prototyped packaging experiments, I designed a final origami-style fold package and logo, then tested the prototype with real customers in a grocery store. July to August 2024, individual project.
