Hi, I’m Bella.

I study Informatics at UW, and I care about the small moments where something either makes sense to a person or it doesn’t. Most of how I think about design comes from three places.

Seattle, Washington 西雅图

I’m working toward a B.S. in Informatics with a minor in Applied Mathematics at the University of Washington, expected 2029. The part I keep coming back to is human-computer interaction, especially the research side: sitting with someone while they think out loud and noticing where they slow down.

That started before UW. On Studdy Buddy I ran think-aloud interviews, affinity mapping, and a Rose, Bud, Thorn session before I drew a single wireframe, then tested Figma prototypes with students and cut down the sign-up steps. On Lullaboo I got into physical computing, wiring an Arduino into a teddy bear so touch gestures could replace reaching for a phone at night.

Toronto, Ontario 多伦多

I grew up in Toronto, and a lot of what I know about designing for real people came from community work there. When you run an art session for a room of kids, you find out fast whether your instructions are clear.

  1. Head, Co.Wishes

    Feb 2023 – Sept 2025

    Recruited and trained volunteers, built partnerships with local organizations, and helped design and build the website for our donation card program.

  2. Lead, Art City Community Partnership

    Sept 2022 – Mar 2025

    Ran arts enrichment programs with local schools for kids aged 9 to 12, adapting the curriculum to each group and coordinating with school administrators.

Language and perspective 语言与视角

I speak English and Mandarin, and I also speak French. Going back and forth between English and Chinese changes how I read a page. Chinese characters sit on an even grid, so spacing and rhythm do a lot of the work that bold type and size do in English.

There’s an idea in Chinese painting called 留白, leaving space on purpose. I try to bring that into interfaces: fewer things on the screen, a clear order, and room for the important part to stand out.
