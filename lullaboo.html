Lullaboo

Year
2024 (April to July)
Type
Individual project
Tools
Figma, Procreate, Arduino, sewing
Size
30 x 20 x 5 cm

A teddy bear alarm clock that makes waking up softer.

Lullaboo is a multifunctional alarm clock built to improve how you fall asleep and wake up, without a phone in your hand. It is a soft, cuddly bear with air quality detectors, plus a voice recorder so you can capture a dream before you forget it.

The app pairs with the bear. It keeps a diary of your dreams, shows your sleep stats, and can use AI to turn a dream into a short film or image you can share with friends.

The product

Every function lives on the bear itself, so you never have to pick up your phone at night.

Problem and insights

Phones keep people up, alarm clocks are not pleasant to wake up to, and dreams disappear within minutes of waking.

Problem statement

  • Distracted by technology before bed
  • Hard, uncomfortable shape of traditional bedside alarm clocks
  • Difficulty capturing and sharing fleeting dreams

Insights

  • Dream recorder with voice activation
  • Soft, cuddly alarm clock
  • App that uses AI to generate dream movies and images for sharing
