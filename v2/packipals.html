PackiPals

Origami-fold takeout packaging that keeps food warm on the way home.

Year
2024, July to August
Role
Individual project
Methods
Material research, paper prototyping
Testing
In-store, with real customers
(01)

Overview

Takeout food loses heat fast on the way home. I framed a How Might We question around keeping it warm, researched how heat escapes takeout containers, and compared insulating materials.

After two rounds of paper-prototyped packaging experiments, I designed a final origami-style fold package and logo, then tested the prototype with real customers in a grocery store.
