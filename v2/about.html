About

Hi, I’m Bella.

I study Informatics at UW, and I care about the small moments where something either makes sense to a person or it doesn’t.

(01)

Seattle

I’m a second-year student working toward a B.S. in Informatics on the Data Science track, with a minor in Applied Mathematics, at the University of Washington. I expect to graduate in 2029.

The part I keep coming back to is research: sitting with someone while they think out loud and noticing where they slow down. I’m learning to back that up with data, so design decisions rest on more than a hunch. My coursework so far covers data programming in Python, R, and databases with SQL.

On Studdy Buddy I ran think-aloud interviews, affinity mapping, and a Rose, Bud, Thorn session before drawing a wireframe. On Lullaboo I got into physical computing, wiring an Arduino into a teddy bear so touch gestures could replace reaching for a phone at night.

(02)

Toronto

I grew up in Toronto, and a lot of what I know about designing for real people came from community work there. When you run an art session for a room of kids, you find out fast whether your instructions are clear.

  1. Leader, Co.Wishes

    Recruited and trained volunteers, built partnerships with local organizations, and helped design and build the website for our donation card program.

    Feb 2023 – Sep 2025

  2. Lead, Art City Community Partnership

    Ran arts enrichment programs with local schools for kids aged 9 to 12, adapting the curriculum to each group and coordinating with school administrators.

    Sep 2022 – Mar 2025
(03)

Language and perspective

I speak English, Mandarin, and French. Going back and forth between English and Chinese changes how I read a page. Chinese characters sit on an even grid, so spacing and rhythm do a lot of the work that bold type and size do in English.

There’s an idea in Chinese painting called 留白, leaving space on purpose. I try to bring that into interfaces: fewer things on the screen, a clear order, and room for the important part to stand out.

(04)

Outside class

Baking and cooking, drawing, music festivals, thrifting and reselling secondhand finds, and planning trips. I also volunteer: animal welfare, arts and culture, and education are the causes I care most about.
