Lullaboo

A teddy bear alarm clock that makes falling asleep and waking up softer, with no phone in your hand.

Year
2024
Role
Individual project
Tools
Figma, Procreate, Arduino, sewing
Size
30 × 20 × 5 cm
(01)

Overview

Lullaboo is a soft, cuddly bear that works as a sleep timer and dream recorder, built on Arduino. It tracks body temperature to estimate sleep quality, and a voice recorder lets you capture a dream before you forget it.

The companion app keeps a diary of your dreams, shows sleep stats, and can use AI to turn a dream into a short film or image to share.

Problem and insights

Phones keep people up, alarm clocks aren’t pleasant to wake up to, and dreams disappear minutes after waking.

  • Distracted by technology before bed→Every control lives on the bear, not the phone
  • Hard, uncomfortable bedside alarm clocks→A soft, cuddly alarm clock
  • Dreams are hard to capture and share→Voice-activated recorder and AI dream films
