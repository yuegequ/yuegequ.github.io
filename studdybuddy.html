Ditch the stress, ace the test.

After hearing friends talk about school stress and mismatched schedules, I designed Studdy Buddy. The app automatically groups students by when they want to study, which removes the back-and-forth of organizing a study group.

I also added tracking features, which received positive feedback in pilot tests with peers.

The product

The three features that set Studdy Buddy apart.

123
01
Find a Buddy

Automatically identifies friends with a similar time availability, so setting a time is easier.

02
Chat box

A temporary chat to set a study location. It closes after both people confirm the task is done, to prevent distractions. History is saved.

03
Reward system

After completing tasks or earning study-hour achievements, collect points to spend on dressing your Buddy profile icon.

The issue

Stress is common among students, and the students I talked to said it was connected to school.

45.1%

of students report tremendous stress, according to Research.com.

78%

of the students I interviewed named school as one of their main stressors.

Research

Interviews and a persona shaped the problem statements.

Interviews

I interviewed 7 people at different high schools and synthesized what they said.

"...multiple assignments piling up really increases students stress, especially if they can't organize it properly or they have other commitments, like sports..."Ms. Whites, school nurse
"...stigma against mental health makes it really hard to reach out for support... I'd rather get academic help and decrease my stress there..."Tyler, Grade 12 student

Persona

Emily Wang

Junior year, 16 years old. Plays sports, many extracurriculars, social.

Frustrations

  • Overwhelming workload
  • Disconnected from friends
  • Running out of time

Wants and needs

  • Academic help
  • Peer connections
  • Time management

How students cope (rose, buds and thorns)

  • Working and chatting with friends, in person or over the phone
  • Meditation, including breathing techniques and following a tutorial
  • Hobbies, arts and crafts, and music

Problem statements and insights

  • Need to organize assignments and other commitments→Flexible calendar or task management system
  • Meeting deadlines→Automated deadline alerts or progress tracker
  • Working with friends but struggling to set a time→Group time-matching features or real-time availability status
